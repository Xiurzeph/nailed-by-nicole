Booking Boundaries

Studio Guidelines & Policies

Please review our guidelines thoroughly prior to requesting a booking slot. These enforce respect for scheduling and product health.

⏱️

Punctuality Rule

To maintain our timeline blocks, there is a strict 10-minute grace window. Arrivals beyond this point may lead to shortened service options or automatic cancellation, incurring the full booking charge.

📅

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations, schedule transfers, or structural reductions must be requested at least 24 hours prior to scheduled starts. Standard late cancellations may be subject to penalty processing charges.

🛡️

Foreign Work Terms

To maintain standard health hygiene and product safety, we do not refill or structural-blend over work from other establishments. First-time guests must secure a full clean removal if coming with existing enhancements.
